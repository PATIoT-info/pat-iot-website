PAT Energy Calculator: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

PAT Energy Calculator is an Android app published by PAT IoT Solutions. It helps you estimate how much electricity your appliances use and what that may cost. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information the app stores

The app saves the following on your device so that it is still there the next time you open the app:

  • the appliance names you enter, with their wattage, daily hours of use and quantity
  • the electricity rate you enter

Information we collect

We do not collect any of this information. The app has no user accounts, does not send your entries to us or to anyone else, and does not contain advertising or analytics.

Sharing

Because the app does not collect your information, we do not sell it or share it with third parties.

Deleting your information

You can delete an appliance from inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes everything it has stored on your device.

Google Play

You download the app through Google Play, which is operated by Google. Google handles information about your use of Play under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date.
