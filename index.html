About Us

PAT IoT Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a leading home automation company dedicated to transforming ordinary homes into intelligent living spaces. We specialize in manufacturing premium smart touch switch boards, DALI touch dimming systems, and PWM modules.

Based in Hyderabad, Telangana, our team of certified professionals works closely with clients to design and implement customized automation systems that perfectly match their lifestyle and budget.

Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted name in the Indian smart home automation industry.
