PAT IoT App

PAT IoT App lets you control and personalize your entire home from anywhere with ease. Designed for simplicity and complete security, the app allows you to manage lights, fans, curtains, and other smart devices in real time through a single intuitive interface.

You can use the app across multiple devices with no limits, add real photos of your home to each room for a more personalized experience, and enjoy full end-to-end encryption for total peace of mind. With seamless customization, instant responsiveness, and smooth performance, PAT IoT App delivers a reliable and effortless smart living experience—making your home smarter, safer, and more comfortable every day.

About Us

PAT IoT Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a leading home automation company dedicated to transforming ordinary homes into intelligent living spaces. We specialize in manufacturing premium smart touch switch boards, DALI dimming systems, PWM modules, analog dimming, and phase cut dimming, while also providing comprehensive smart home automation solutions.

Based in Hyderabad, Telangana, our team of certified professionals works closely with clients to design and implement customized automation systems that perfectly match their lifestyle and budget. We combine our own manufactured products with carefully selected third-party solutions to deliver complete home automation experiences.

Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted name in the Indian smart home automation industry.
